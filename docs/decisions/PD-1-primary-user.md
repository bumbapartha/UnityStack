# PD-1 — Primary user: the founder, dogfooding

[← Decisions](README.md)

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 27 November 2026

## Context

UnityStack is aimed at organisations eventually, but it is at an early stage and built by one person.

## Decision

For the next 6–12 months the founder is the sole user and developer, building UnityStack with UnityStack. Later come free demos with a few developers or organisations, and enterprise buyers after that.

## Consequences

Enterprise needs (installer, SSO, HA, onboarding, backfill) stay as direction, not current design constraints; they are P2.
