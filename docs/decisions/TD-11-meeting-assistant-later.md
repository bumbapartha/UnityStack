# TD-11 — AI meeting assistant as a future capability

[← Decisions](README.md)

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 27 November 2026

## Context

Requirement meetings with developers, product and testers would benefit from an AI participant.

## Decision

Plan an AI meeting assistant that joins calls, asks questions, summarises and later proposes requirement updates as PRs. Not built now.

## Consequences

P2; meeting platform still open (Jitsi Meet or Mattermost calls, with Zoom and Teams adapters).
